How it works ✕

Infantryfree, broad wave · beats Raiders head-on · stopped by Forts
Artilleryslow, shells the enemy army every second · ignores Forts · beats Tanks
Raidersnarrow fast spear deep inland · steals gold · beats Artillery
Tankscheap per tile, heavy punch · beats Infantry · hate Bunkers & Artillery
Fort×2 cost for Infantry & Raiders nearby
Bunkersmall, ×1.6–1.8 vs Artillery & Tanks
Minefieldhidden; blows 45% off the first attack through it
Air defenseblocks Air strikes nearby (whole team); 1 interceptor per level - 2 loaded interceptors shoot down a Nuke, then reload
City · Market · Minetroops / gold; +15% per liked neighbour; land within 4 tiles never revolts; captured intact
Air strikewrecks buildings, wipes land, guts Artillery
Nuke6000g, only 2 loaded air-defence interceptors stop it: wipes a big circle, fallout blocks it for minutes, the whole world turns on you
Home guardwhen attacked, your nation automatically fights back with infantry on that front (up to ~35% of your troops); it bleeds the attackers, loses to tanks, beats raiders
Mercenariesspare gold → troops (send % slider), up to max troops
Barbed wirecheap, 1 tile: Infantry ×1.5, Raiders ×1.7 — Tanks roll over it
Hedgehogscheap, 1 tile: Tanks ×2.2
Terrainforest, hills, marsh, mountains and rivers slow every unit and cost more troops — Tanks hate forest, marsh and mountains
Spearheadsa tile with enemies on most sides costs up to +80% troops; land cut off from its main body (hatched) costs half
Stabilitywar, new fronts, air strikes, broken pacts and overreach drain it; peace, pacts and economy rebuild it. Protests → Strikes → Riots → Revolution: less gold & growth, revolts, wrecked buildings
Prisonersattackers who fall on your land: 5% join you (20% under any defence); a broken attack surrenders half of what's left
DepositsOil (Oil well: lots of gold, runs dry), Coal & Iron ore (Mine; iron makes units 15% cheaper each, max 45%), Black earth (more gold and troop growth just for holding it). Fields run dry faster with more extractors
SeasonsWinter: rivers & marshes freeze (cross like plains), everything moves 40% slower, less growth. Mud after winter: tanks & artillery half speed on open ground
Rivers & supplyattacking across a river costs +25% (not in winter). Far from your capital with no hub/port nearby: +20% (stretched supply)
Numbersfloating numbers show losses, prisoners, loot and income — P turns them off
Universityresearch points: each level +4% growth & gold, +3% attack & defence, +0.5% hub tolls. Universities linked by your trade network (roads, hubs, ports — even a peaceful neighbour's) give +25% each
Portcoast only: fishing gold, and shipping lanes to any port within 60 tiles — trade across the sea
Encirclementenemy land cut off from its main body (hatched): only 30% cost and its defenders surrender to you. Your own cut-off land pays no gold and its garrison wastes away — half of it flees as refugees to whoever surrounds it
Logistics hubrelays trade between Markets; takes 3/4/5% of every route through it (by level). Railway: links rail hubs far apart if you have a pact
Upgradeclick your building: level 2–3 = +60% output each, defences grow their range
Diplomacyright-click a nation (or its leaderboard row): Trade · Non-aggression · Tribute · Defensive · Alliance · Eternal. Breaking costs gold, deep pacts also troops (they desert to the victim), a traitor mark and trade

How to play

Click free land to spawn. Then click any neighbouring land to attack with the share of troops set on the slider — troops regrow fastest when you are not maxed out. Forts stop infantry, artillery breaks forts, raiders strike deep, air strikes hit anything. Huge empires get unstable: keep your army above 70% of max or far provinces revolt. Win by holding the target share of land, or by leading when time runs out.

PACTA. Eastern Europe & Baltics maps: real geography from Natural Earth (public domain), relief and some rivers drawn by hand. All nations are fictional.
